Manual entry · BPM is not available over MIDI.

Choose a local copy of the current TP-7 track to read its saved BPM, or enter it manually. Files stay on your computer.

Before Apply: start playback on TP-7 and set its on-device SPD to 1×, with external clock sync off. Apply resets the rocker and sends the closest MIDI speed immediately. The TP-7 screen's SPD stays unchanged.

The preview uses this project's experimental speed mapping. MIDI has finite precision; the displayed BPM is a prediction, not a measurement from TP-7.
